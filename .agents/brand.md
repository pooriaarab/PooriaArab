# Pooria Arab Brand

Pooria Arab is an AI-native builder. His work centers on agent tools,
local-first systems, creator software, and fast product experiments.

The profile line is “Vibing everyday.” Use it as a casual status, not a product
claim. Refer to the person as “Pooria” in conversational copy and “Pooria Arab”
in formal identification.

## Voice

- Write for builders who understand software and AI.
- Use direct, active sentences and concrete project names.
- Keep the tone casual, curious, and technically precise.
- Let playful phrases, such as peanut butter references, stay occasional.
- Avoid corporate language, inflated claims, and generic AI slogans.

## Themes

- Building and sharing practical tools.
- Portable agent workflows and reusable skills.
- Local-first and peer-to-peer software.
- Vibe coding, startups, AI, and the creator economy.

## Naming

Preserve each project’s published spelling and capitalization. Many project
names use a lowercase `vibe` prefix. This pattern describes the current
portfolio, but it is not a rule for every new project.

## External assets

Social badges, the profile-view counter, and the contribution graph belong to
their providers. Their colors and shapes are not Pooria’s brand assets.
